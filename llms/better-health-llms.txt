# Better Health

> Better Health (Better Health Supplies) is a modern direct-to-consumer medical supplier shipping home medical supplies for chronic conditions — diabetes and CGMs, incontinence, urology and self-catheterization, ostomy, and wound care — directly to patients, paired with insurance-benefit navigation and peer/clinical support. Backed by General Catalyst. Storefront on Shopify at joinbetter.com. This company publishes a consumer commerce experience, not a first-party developer API.
